Terms of Service Privacy Policy
Is this open-source software?

No. Mac Watch is paid commercial software. Neither the app source nor this website is released under an open-source license, and redistribution or resale is not permitted.

What happens when a Yearly license expires?

Nothing breaks. The app keeps every feature you already have — updates simply pause until you renew ($4.9 / ¥29.9). Lifetime licenses include all future updates and never pause.

Which systems are supported?

Core features support macOS 14+. Newer system capabilities such as Liquid Glass enhance the experience when available, but never gate core features.

Does the music feature include content rights?

No. The app only provides playback and control; you are responsible for your accounts, content, and compliance with local regulations.

Is it related to Apple Watch?

There is no official relationship. Apple, Apple Watch, and macOS are trademarks of Apple Inc.; Mac Watch is independent software.
